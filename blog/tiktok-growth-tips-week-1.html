Week 1 · TikTok Growth

TikTok Par Views Kam Aane Ki 5 Aam Wajahain

Agar aapki TikTok video par views kam aa rahe hain, to pareshan hone se pehle ye samajhna zaroori hai ke wajah kya hai. Zyada tar cases mein reason koi bara "algorithm secret" nahi hota — balke ye 5 chhoti chhoti cheezein hoti hain jo repeat hoti rehti hain.

1. Pehle 3 Second Boring Hain

TikTok ka algorithm dekhta hai log video kitni jaldi scroll kar jate hain. Agar shuru ke 3 second mein kuch bhi interesting nahi hota — koi movement, koi surprising line, koi visual change — to log scroll kar jate hain, aur video ko kam log dikhaye jate hain. Fix: video ka sabse interesting hissa sabse pehle rakhein, chahe wo end mein tha.

2. Generic Hashtags Use Karna

#fyp aur #viral daal dena kaafi nahi hai — ye hashtags itne overused hain ke inme aapki video kisi bhi specific audience tak nahi pohanchti. Niche-specific hashtags (jaise apni city, apni field ke naam se) zyada targeted logon tak video pohanchate hain.

3. Ghalat Waqt Par Post Karna

Agar aapki audience Pakistan mein hai aur aap dopeher 2 baje post karte hain, to zyada log online nahi hote. Shaam 7 se 10 baje ka waqt generally behtar hota hai jab log office/school se free ho chuke hote hain.

4. Irregular Posting

Kabhi ek din mein 2 videos, phir 5 din ka gap — ye pattern algorithm ko confuse karta hai. Consistent schedule (jaise hafte mein 3-4 din) behtar result deta hai.

5. Caption Mein Koi Call-to-Action Na Hona

Sirf video dikhana kaafi nahi — log ko batayein wo kya karein: "comment karein", "follow karein", "aisi aur videos ke liye save karein". Ye engagement badhata hai, jo algorithm ko positive signal deta hai.

Aage Kya Karein

Agar aap apni specific video check karna chahte hain, hamara Caption Improver aur Hashtag Generator abhi free istemal kar sakte hain.
