Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Hum Kya Data Collect Karte Hain

TrackFyp ke free tools (Hashtag Generator, Best Time to Post, Caption Improver) aapke browser mein hi chalte hain — jo bhi text ya link aap type karte hain, wo humare kisi server par nahi bheja jata aur na hi kahin store hota hai.

Aapki language choice (Roman Urdu / English) sirf aapke apne browser mein (local storage) yaad rakhi jati hai, taake agli baar wahi language khule. Ye data bhi humare paas nahi aata.

Jab Backend Wale Tools Live Honge

Jab Video/Channel Analysis, Account Health, Shadowban Checker jaise tools live honge (jo TikTok ka public data fetch karte hain), tab sirf wo link/username process hoga jo aap khud submit karein — sirf public information dikhane ke liye. Ye policy update kar di jayegi jab ye tools live hon, taake pura clear ho ke kya data kitni der ke liye store hota hai.

Cookies Aur Ads

Agar future mein is website par Google AdSense ke zariye ads dikhayi jayen, to Google aur uske partners cookies use kar sakte hain taake relevant ads dikhayi ja sakein. Aap apne browser settings se cookies control kar sakte hain, aur Google ki apni privacy policy yahan dekh sakte hain.

Third-Party Links

Hamari website kabhi kabhi doosri websites (jaise TikTok) ka link deti hai. Un websites ki apni alag privacy policies hoti hain, jinki hum zimmedari nahi lete.

Rabta Karein

Agar is policy ke baare mein koi sawaal ho, hamein Contact page ke zariye rabta karein.
